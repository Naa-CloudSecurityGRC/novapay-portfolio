# Stakeholder Interview Guide: SOX Evidence Collection

**Purpose:** understand how control owners collect and submit quarterly SOX evidence, where time is lost, and what would help.
**Format:** 30-minute, one-on-one, semi-structured. Notes taken live; quotes kept word for word.

## Opening (3 min)
- Thank them; explain the goal is to improve the process, not to audit them.
- Ask permission to take notes; confirm comments are summarized without names.

## Role and context (5 min)
1. Which controls do you own, and how many evidence requests do you get each quarter?
2. Who else is involved in producing the evidence?

## Current process (10 min)
3. Walk me through the last evidence request you received, step by step.
4. Where do you get the evidence from (systems, reports, emails)?
5. How do you know what "good evidence" looks like for each request?
6. How long did that request take, start to finish? How much of that was waiting?

## Pain points (7 min)
7. What is the most frustrating part of the process?
8. Tell me about a request that was sent back. What happened?
9. If you could change one thing, what would it be?

## Wrap-up (5 min)
10. Is there anyone else I should speak to?
11. Can I follow up with you to validate what I heard?
