# Information Security Policy

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Policy ID | POL-01 |
| Owner | CISO |
| Approved by | Chief Information Security Officer (CISO); Audit & Risk Committee notified |
| Effective date | 2026-12-01 |
| Review cycle | Annual, or after a significant change |
| Applies to | All NovaPay employees, contractors and third parties with access to NovaPay systems or data |

> NovaPay is a fictional company. This policy is a portfolio sample.

## 1. Purpose
Set NovaPay's commitment to protect the confidentiality, integrity and availability of customer, cardholder and company information, and establish the framework that all other security policies sit under.

## 2. Scope
All information NovaPay creates, processes or stores, in any form, and all systems, networks, devices and cloud services used to handle it.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Board Audit & Risk Committee | Oversees security risk; receives a security report each quarter |
| CISO | Owns the security program and this policy; reports risks and incidents |
| GRC Lead | Maintains policies, risk register and control matrix; runs compliance monitoring |
| System and data owners | Classify data, approve access, and make sure controls operate for their systems |
| All workforce members | Follow policies, complete training, report incidents and suspicious activity |

## 4. Policy statements
1. **Risk management:** NovaPay assesses information security risk at least annually and after significant changes, and treats risks above appetite with a documented plan.
2. **Policies and standards:** supporting policies (POL-02 to POL-05) and technical standards are approved by the CISO and reviewed annually.
3. **Data classification:** information is classified as Restricted (e.g., cardholder data, government IDs), Confidential, Internal or Public, and protected according to its class.
4. **Asset management:** all hardware, software and cloud assets are recorded in an inventory with a named owner.
5. **Access control:** access follows least privilege and is governed by POL-03.
6. **Encryption:** Restricted and Confidential data is encrypted at rest and in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher).
7. **Secure change:** changes to production follow an approved change management process.
8. **Vulnerability management:** systems are scanned at least weekly; critical vulnerabilities are fixed within 15 days and high within 30 days.
9. **Logging and monitoring:** security-relevant events are logged centrally and reviewed.
10. **Incidents:** suspected incidents are reported and handled under POL-04.
11. **Third parties:** vendors with access to NovaPay data or systems are assessed before onboarding and reviewed according to their risk tier.
12. **Training:** the workforce completes security awareness training on hire and annually.
13. **Compliance:** NovaPay meets legal, regulatory and contractual requirements, including PCI DSS, GLBA and state privacy laws.

## 5. Exceptions
Exceptions must be requested in writing, include the risk and compensating controls, be approved by the CISO, and expire after no more than 12 months. The GRC Lead keeps the exception register.

## 6. Enforcement
Violations may lead to disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment or contract.
