# Acceptable Use Policy

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Policy ID | POL-02 |
| Owner | GRC Lead |
| Approved by | Chief Information Security Officer (CISO); Audit & Risk Committee notified |
| Effective date | 2026-12-01 |
| Review cycle | Annual, or after a significant change |
| Applies to | All NovaPay employees, contractors and third parties with access to NovaPay systems or data |

> NovaPay is a fictional company. This policy is a portfolio sample.

## 1. Purpose
Define how NovaPay's systems, devices and data may be used, to protect customers, the workforce and the company.

## 2. Scope
All NovaPay-owned or managed devices, accounts, email, collaboration tools, networks and cloud services, and any personal device used for NovaPay work.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| All workforce members | Use resources as this policy allows; sign the acknowledgment on hire and annually |
| Managers | Make sure their teams understand and follow this policy |
| IT Operations | Provide approved tools; enforce technical controls (e.g., device management) |

## 4. Policy statements
1. NovaPay resources are for business use. Limited personal use is allowed if it doesn't interfere with work, break the law or create security risk.
2. Users are responsible for activity on their accounts and must not share passwords or MFA devices.
3. Only NovaPay-approved software and cloud services may be used for company data. New tools are requested through IT.
4. Restricted data (e.g., cardholder or government ID data) must never be sent by personal email, stored on personal devices or entered into unapproved AI tools.
5. Devices must have screen lock (5 minutes or less), full-disk encryption and endpoint protection, and be locked when unattended.
6. Users must not try to bypass security controls, access data they aren't authorized for, or install unapproved remote-access tools.
7. Suspected phishing, lost devices or other incidents must be reported to security@novapay.example immediately.
8. NovaPay may monitor its systems and accounts for security and compliance, as permitted by law. Users should have no expectation of privacy on company systems.
9. Prohibited at all times: harassment, illegal content, cryptocurrency mining, and any activity that violates the law or NovaPay's Code of Conduct.

## 5. Exceptions
Requests go to the GRC Lead using the exception process in POL-01.

## 6. Enforcement
Violations may lead to removal of access and disciplinary action, up to termination.
