# Privacy Analyst Projects

| # | Project | Key artifacts |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Data Inventory & RoPA](01-ropa-data-inventory/) | 10 processing activities (GDPR Art. 30), data-flow diagram, legal-basis and transfer analysis |
| 2 | [DPIA: AI Fraud Scoring](02-dpia-ai-fraud-scoring/) | Full DPIA: necessity, Article 22 automated decisions, model-bias risk, residual risk sign-off |
| 3 | [DSAR Playbook](03-dsar-playbook/) | GDPR and CCPA access/deletion process, verification, exemptions, templates, deadline tracker |
| 4 | [Privacy Notice & Cookie Audit Toolkit](04-privacy-cookie-audit/) | 18-point checklist, cookie scan script, report template (audit to be completed on a live site) |

The RoPA flags the fraud-scoring activity as high risk, which leads to the DPIA; the DSAR playbook uses the RoPA's system list as its search checklist.
