# Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM) Projects

| # | Project | Key artifacts |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [SOC 2 Type II Report Review](01-soc2-report-review/) | Review memo: scope, carve-outs, 3 exceptions, CUEC mapping, bridge letter, Moderate residual risk |
| 2 | [Due-Diligence Questionnaire](02-due-diligence-questionnaire/) | 40 weighted questions, critical knockouts, scorecard with formula-driven rating |
| 3 | [Fourth-Party & Concentration Risk Map](03-fourth-party-concentration/) | Dependency map, concentration findings, contract clauses, exit plans |
| 4 | [Continuous Monitoring Alerts](04-continuous-monitoring/) | 3 vendor incident alerts with exposure, actions and vendor questions |

Built around the third-party lifecycle in the 2023 Interagency Guidance on Third-Party Relationships: due diligence (2), contract terms and exit (3), ongoing monitoring (1, 4).
